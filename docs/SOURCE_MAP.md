# SOURCE_MAP.md

Provenance record for the seven source documents behind ARETE.
Rebuilt 2026-09-09 by direct text extraction (`pdftotext -layout`) of the files in
`Reset-Program/App in prgress/`. 143 pages total.

**Rule for every content pack in this app:** each item carries a `source` and
`sourceLicense` field. Nothing in this map may be shipped verbatim unless its row says so.

## 1. `66_Day_Reset_CALM_SLEEK_LINKED.pdf` — 38 pages

Titled **"60-Day Reset – Calm and Sleek"**. Contains **66 numbered days**.

### THE 60-vs-66 INCONSISTENCY (must never be silently resolved)

The document's title says 60 days. The day-by-day content runs Day 1 → Day 66, and the
final page's recommendation reads "Gentle deload after Day 66."

**Decision (revised in stage 11, ledger D96):** ARETE runs a **60-day programme** — the
number in the source's title — and offers the source's **days 61–66 as an optional
"extension week"**, off by default, which the person can add before starting or at the finish.
Nothing beyond day 60 is imported silently, and the screen says, in one line, that the PDF is
titled 60 days but runs 66. (Stages 1–10 planned to ship it as "66-Day Reset" with a 60-day
truncation; Prompt 11 asked for the extension-week treatment instead, and it is the clearer one.)
The app must never render a 60-day title over 66 days of content.

### Structure (extracted, machine-readable)

Five week-blocks, each with a header line of daily parameters, then per-day timed schedules,
then a summary table. The summary tables yield a clean 66-row dataset — captured to
`src/content/reset/reset-66-day.json`.

| Block | Days  | Wake  | Water | Study     | Social cap         |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | --------- | ------------------ |
| 1     | 1–7   | 07:00 | 2 L   | 1 h/day   | 30 m               |
| 2     | 8–21  | 07:00 | 2.5 L | 1 h/day   | 20 m               |
| 3     | 22–42 | 06:30 | 3 L   | 1–2 h/day | 15 m → 10 m        |
| 4     | 43–56 | 06:00 | 3.5 L | 1 h/day   | 10 m               |
| 5     | 57–66 | 06:00 | 4 L   | 1 h/day   | mixed, down to 0 m |

Per-day columns: `wake, water, workout, pushups, run, meditation, reading, study, coldShower, social`.

Progression endpoints: run 2 km → 10 km; push-ups 10 → 100; gym 60 → 120 min;
meditation 5 → 25 min; reading 10 → 30 pages.

### Safety and product issues

- **Escalating targets are aggressive and untailored.** 10 km runs, 100 push-ups, 120-minute
  gym sessions and 4 L/day water are prescribed by calendar date, not by the user's baseline.
  4 L/day is above typical guidance for most adults and is a real hyponatremia consideration
  at the high end combined with heavy sweating.
- **Wake times (07:00 → 06:00) are hardcoded** and conflict with the other reset PDF (06:00)
  and with any user who works nights.
- **Cold shower is a near-daily checkbox** with no screening, no opt-out, no contraindications.
- **Social media is driven to 0 m** — presented as virtue rather than a user choice.

**As built (stage 11):** `domain/reset-program.ts` keeps the source's components and its five
blocks (renamed Foundation, Build, Steady, Stretch, Consolidate) and replaces every number with
a suggested range worked out from the person: water from body weight and climate (30–35 ml/kg,
capped at 3.5 L, never the source's 4 L), movement from the session length they gave Train
(capped at 90 minutes, gentler with a pain flag or as a beginner), study scaled down for busy
weekdays, meditation from their stated experience, a wake window only around a time they chose,
and 7–9 hours' sleep. The source's own numbers are shown beside each block, for reference.

**Product consequence:** the reset is an _optional, editable program template_. Every parameter
(wake, water, run distance, push-up count, gym duration, meditation length, social cap,
cold exposure) is a default the user can change, scale, or remove at onboarding and mid-program.
Cold exposure and fasting-adjacent targets are **opt-in**, never seeded on by default.

### Rights

Generated by the user for personal use; embedded links point to third-party YouTube content
(Cal Newport, Huberman, Attia). **Do not embed or reproduce third-party video content.**
Store links as external references only, clearly attributed, opened out of app.

## 2. `60_Day_Reset_Pamphlet.pdf` — 2 pages

Quick-reference sheet. Wake 07:00, hydrate, workout, 1 h study, 10–20 pages reading,
5–25 min meditation, cold shower, social ≤ 20 m.
10-week progression table: run 2 → 8–10 km, push-ups 20 → 60, gym 1 h → 2 h, meditation 5 → 25 m.

**Conflicts with #1:** social cap 20 m (vs 30 m), push-ups start at 20 (vs 10), gym tops at 2 h
(vs 2 h at day 58 in #1), 10 weeks (vs 66 days ≈ 9.4 weeks).

Useful, non-controversial content worth keeping: "Prioritize 7–9 h sleep", "one long walk per
week", "journal 2–3 lines each evening", and — importantly for tone — **"If you miss a day,
reset immediately"**, which the app reframes as _a missed day does not erase progress_.

## 3. `60_day_reset_regimen.pdf` — 2 pages

A **different program for a different person**: explicitly "for a 17-year-old tennis player."
Wake 06:00, cold plunge 3 min + steam 10 min at 06:30, school 07:30–17:20, PPL workout 18:00,
sauna + cold shower 18:45, 1.5 h homework, lights out 22:30.
4 phases over 8 weeks: Form → Strength → Intensity → Performance.

### Issues

- **Minor-specific.** A schedule built around a school day must not be presented to an adult
  user as a default, and the app's primary user is an adult.
- Contains **five unattributed motivational quotes** ("Your body achieves what your mind
  believes", "Consistency beats intensity", etc.). Per the product rule on quotes, these are
  **not shippable** — no verified attribution exists. Either drop them or label them clearly
  as unattributed common sayings. Do not invent an author.
- Daily cold plunge + sauna for a teenager, unscreened.

**Product consequence:** this file contributes the **Push/Pull/Legs 4-phase progression shape**
and nothing else. Its schedule is not a template.

## 4. `Contrast_Therapy_Schedule.pdf` — 1 page

Two protocols plus a weekly pairing table.

| Protocol                        | Heat                         | Cold                       | Cycles | Finish         |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------ | -------------- |
| Muscle strength / growth        | 160–180 °F (70–82 °C), 5 min | 55 °F (13 °C), 1 min       | 3–4    | heat, 5–10 min |
| Athletic performance / recovery | 104–110 °F (40–43 °C), 3 min | 50–59 °F (10–15 °C), 1 min | 4      | cold, 2–3 min  |

Weekly table pairs Mon/Thu strength → growth routine (evening, 6–8 h post-workout),
Tue/Fri cardio → performance routine (immediately post), Wed mobility, Sat optional, Sun rest.

### Safety — the highest-risk content in the set

Sauna at 70–82 °C and cold water at 13 °C carry genuine risk: syncope, arrhythmia,
cold-shock response, and interaction with cardiovascular conditions, pregnancy, blood-pressure
medication, and alcohol. The source gives **no contraindications and no screening**.

**Product consequence:** contrast therapy is a **strictly opt-in module, off by default,**
gated behind an explicit safety screen that lists contraindications, states this is not
medical advice, recommends never doing cold immersion alone, and offers conservative defaults
(shorter, milder) below the source's numbers. The rationale strings from the source
("heat-shock proteins", "growth hormone", "reduce inflammation") are **mechanistic claims
presented without citation** — restate them as "commonly cited rationale", never as effects
the app promises.

**As built (stage 11, ledger D100–D102):** Programmes → Contrast therapy. Off until the person
answers an eight-question screening (pre-ticked from onboarding where it already knows) and
acknowledges a versioned safety notice. Any yes means "talk to a doctor first", and the timer
stays off unless they say a doctor has cleared them; under 18 it is not offered at all. The
default protocol is 3 minutes' heat, 30 seconds' cool, two rounds, finishing warm — below both
source protocols, which are shown for reference with their rationale labelled as commonly cited.
"Stop now" is on screen throughout; stopping for dizziness says to sit down and seek help. No
session counts towards a streak or a reset day. Turning it off deletes the screening.

## 5. `Jewish_Meditation_4_Week_Program.pdf` — 1 page

Four weeks: (1) breath awareness, stopping thoughts; (2) visualizing the Hebrew letter Aleph,
object contemplation; (3) Shema meditation, Divine Name / Psalm 16:8 "Shiviti Hashem lenegdi
tamid"; (4) Hitbodedut personal prayer, unification meditation.
Practice notes: consistency over length, quiet environment, short journal after each session.

### Rights and product rule

Liturgical text (Shema, Psalm 16:8) is public domain; transliterations here are conventional.
This is safe to ship **as an optional content pack**.

**The product rule is explicit and non-negotiable:** this pack is **never** enabled by default,
never inferred from the user's name, language, region, or any other signal. It appears only in
a neutral "meditation packs" list that the user opens and chooses from, alongside secular packs.

## 6. `The-Complete-Seed-Oil-Guide-Joseph-Everett.pdf` — 27 pages

A **Substack article by Joseph Everett (WIL)**, Oct 2024, printed to PDF
(`josepheverettwil.substack.com/p/the-complete-seed-oil-guide`).

Structure: Step 1 don't eat fried foods · Step 2 don't cook with seed oils · Step 3 stop using
mayonnaise and oil-based dressings · Step 4 beware of blends · 5 read labels · note on
restaurants · "how to recover" (eat more saturated fat, etc.).

### Rights — the most restrictive item in the set

**Third-party copyrighted work, all rights reserved, no license to redistribute.**
It may not be bundled, quoted at length, paraphrased section-by-section, or shipped as an
in-app guide. It contains embedded images and screenshots that are also third-party material.

### Editorial position

This is **an advocacy piece, not a consensus summary.** Its central claims — that seed-oil
PUFA oxidation drives disease and that people should increase saturated fat — are contested
by major dietary guidance bodies. It reasons from animal studies (hogs, rodents, alcohol-liver
models) to human dietary advice.

**Product consequence:** ARETE's nutrition module does **not** adopt a diet ideology.
The seed-oil material informs at most one optional, clearly-labelled "cooking-oil awareness"
note that presents it as _one contested viewpoint_, names the author, links out to the original,
and states that mainstream guidance differs. The uncontroversial, non-ideological behaviour it
suggests — read ingredient labels, know what is in a blend — is fine to surface on its own.
The nutrition MVP is manual calorie/macro logging with no diet doctrine attached.

**As built (stage 10, ledger D92):** an optional "ingredient and oil awareness" preference,
off by default, under Food → Targets & settings. It holds the person's own prefer / limit /
avoid list, cooking oils and ingredient-label notes, plus four fixed restaurant questions.
Four short cards (`src/content/nutrition/oil-awareness.ts`) each set what the guide claims
beside how settled the evidence is and what mainstream guidance says, in ARETE's own words;
nothing is quoted or reproduced. No supplement — vitamin E, NAC or otherwise — and no extra
saturated fat is recommended, and the screen says so. Turning it off deletes the row and
every note with it.

## 7. `Total Flexibility - V2.pdf` — 72 pages

**"Movement by David"** (David Thurin) — **four commercial ebooks bound into one file**:

1. Total Hamstring Mobility 2.0
2. Total Hip Mobility
3. Total Posture & Spinal Mobility
4. Total Shoulder Mobility

Each follows an identical shape: journey/benefits/expected results → **warm-up checklist**
(walking lunges, leg swings, high knees, 30 s jog in place — 2 sets each) → **4-week program,
3 days per week, 3 movements per day** with sets and reps-or-time → philosophy → FAQ →
contact → supplement promotion ("Flexy Joints") with a discount code → **disclaimer**.

### Rights — PERMISSION GRANTED (2026-10-19)

The product owner obtained David Thurin's permission for the Total Flexibility material on
2026-10-19 (ledger D85). The table below is kept as the record of what was weighed; ARETE
still writes its own instructions and draws its own animations, and still does not carry the
author's promotions.

| Element                                                                 | Status                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exercise **names** ("Elephant Walks", "Pancake", "Roll Down")           | Common movement vocabulary — usable                                                     |
| Program **structure** (4 weeks × 3 days × 3 movements, these sets/reps) | Arguably his selection and arrangement — **needs permission or must be re-derived**     |
| Exercise **descriptions** (verbatim text above)                         | His expression — **must be rewritten in ARETE's own words**, never copied               |
| **Photographs and illustrations**                                       | Third-party copyright — **never reproduced.** ARETE draws its own vector/SVG animations |
| **Author name, branding, "Flexy Joints" promotion, discount code**      | Not shipped                                                                             |

**Stage 9 (2026-10-19):** built as a sourced template — the structure above, exactly, with
ARETE-written instructions — behind a rights gate (ledger D77, D78). **Cleared the same day**
when permission arrived (D85, migration 0016).

**Resolved 2026-10-19:** option (a) — written permission obtained by the product owner.

### Safety

Generic disclaimer only. Contains loaded end-range positions (Pancake, Standing Pancake,
Roll Down) that are inappropriate straight away for users with disc issues, acute hamstring
strain, or hypermobility. ARETE gates the program behind a low-readiness / injury check,
offers a regression for every movement, and never counts a skipped movement as failure.
